## Overview

In-home delivery was the first chapter of Amazon Key and the foundation everything else was built on. Working with a team of designers, product partners, and engineers, I helped invent a way for customers to receive packages securely inside their homes using a smart lock and camera, launched in Fall 2017. The patterns, tone, and trust model we established here became the blueprint I later carried into the in-car experience.

## The challenge

The central tension was trust. We were asking people to let a delivery driver, and by extension Amazon, into their home. Every design decision had to reduce anxiety and increase a sense of control, from clear scheduling to visible confirmation of what happened and when.
