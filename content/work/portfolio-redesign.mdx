## The opportunity

The old jonokane.com had served its purpose, but the content had drifted —
rambly, unfocused, and years out of date. This rebuild is a chance to sharpen
the story and set a higher bar for craft.

## Approach

- **Content as MDX.** Each case study is a typed MDX file, validated at build
  time, so the writing lives in version control with no CMS to maintain.
- **Motion as a first-class citizen.** Smooth scroll, scroll-linked
  choreography, and a 3D hero — wired in from day one rather than bolted on.
- **A stack built to last.** Next.js on Vercel, Tailwind for velocity, and a
  content pipeline that scales as the library of work grows.

## What's next

This page is the placeholder that proves the pipeline works end to end. The
real case studies — and the hero that makes people stop scrolling — come next.
