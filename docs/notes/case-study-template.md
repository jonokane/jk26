\#\# Overview  
2 to 4 sentences: what it was, why it mattered, your part in it.

\#\# The challenge  
The core problem, the constraints, what made it hard.

\#\# What I did  
The work itself. Process, key decisions, the moments where your judgment  
changed the outcome. This is the part buyers actually read.

\#\# Outcome  
What shipped, what it moved (metrics if public, qualitative if not),  
what you learned.

\#\# \[Optional\] Selected screens / artifacts  
Captions that explain the decision, not just "here is a screen."
