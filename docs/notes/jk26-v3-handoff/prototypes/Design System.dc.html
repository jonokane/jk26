01

Principles

Type is the interface.

Roboto Flex carries the design. Variable weight and width do the work that color and ornament do elsewhere. Chrome stays minimal; the words are the design.

One accent per section.

Each section owns a single color — green (About), violet (Work), teal (Contact). Never mix accents within a section. The nav mirrors the active accent as a wayfinding signal.

Motion is legible, never decorative.

Every animation earns its place by guiding the read (teleprompter focus, wordmark assembly). All motion respects prefers-reduced-motion.

07

Signature patterns

The three scroll behaviors that define the site. Build these first.

Teleprompter (About)

A 640vh wrapper drives a sticky 100vh stage. Scroll progress maps to a moving focal center (60% of viewport height, ±24% focus range). Per word: weight interpolates 440→780, color mixes grey→accent/ink, opacity and blur fall off with distance. Words above the focus blur harder (7px) than words below (2px) — the "already read" feel. Right rail dots track the active statement.

Horizontal gallery (Work)

Vertical scroll inside a sticky stage translates a horizontal #wtrack in X. 8 headliner cards + a 9th "earlier work" panel (2-col list of 7). Wayfinding against disorientation: locked title, live 01 / 09 counter, and a progress bar (#wprog) pinned bottom. Open decision: horizontal poster view vs a filterable vertical index — hybrid keeps horizontal as the signature and offers the vertical list as the filtered alternate.

Wordmark assembly (Welcome)

On load, letters resolve from scale 2.5 + 9px blur to rest, staggered 0.3 + i×0.08s. Eyebrow leads, tagline + scroll cue follow after the run completes. Reduced-motion jumps straight to the resolved state.

Implementation notes
· Throttle all scroll handlers with requestAnimationFrame.
· Re-measure block centers on resize + document.fonts.ready.
· Scroll-spy = viewport-mid element test, not IntersectionObserver.
· Reveals use IO @ 0.2, unobserve after firing (one-shot).
· Gate teleprompter + wordmark behind prefers-reduced-motion.
